What VoltIQ does

Three engines, one data export

You share historical exports — feeder availability sheets, billing and vending records. VoltIQ does the rest, and gives you numbers your team can verify.

NERC Band A Compliance

Reads your availability template, measures each feeder's supply hours against its band's requirement, separates genuine faults from unreadable data, and produces a NERC-ready evidence pack.

Now live

Theft & Loss Detection

Flags meters and feeders whose behaviour is consistent with electricity theft or metering loss — as a prioritised review list for your field team to verify. A lead, never an accusation.

Revenue at Risk

Converts delivered energy into naira and — once you supply collection figures — estimates the recoverable revenue gap on each feeder. Loss becomes a number your finance team can act on.

Data protection

Your customers' data, treated as a responsibility
